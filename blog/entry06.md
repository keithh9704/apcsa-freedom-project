# Entry 6
##### 5/29/26
### Content
After completing my [MVP](https://gd.games/games/90c0ca13-8f50-4b3c-a376-b259c06207bb), It was time to presenting my final class presentation and my final expo in the cafeteria in my high school career.
#### Presenting to the class
I was one of the first ones to present since most of the class had AP’S that would be during the presentation days which meant I would be presenting to less people. In hindsight, It seems like a good thing but I want to experience presenting to more people for more experience. Overall, I felt like I presented well by stating how I made my game, the potential flaws and a little bit of comedy when I failed at my own game.


My takeaways for this presentation are
* If necessary/possible, I should have an easy way to quickly show my project and give everyone in the audience what the concept of my game is like.
* Very niche but make my slides more eye-catching because I didn't spend that much time decorating my slides.
#### Presenting at the Expo
I was positioned at the very right back corner so I didn't get that many people to come near my project but when people did approach my table I presented to them. I would get them to answer the question on my game and led them to one of the two decisions on my project. Then I would show them the other option if they were curious about it. For some people, I didn't get to show all the concepts to the audience because of the sudden bugs of the player walking off the platform by itself since the wifi was malfunctioning. When I presented to the judges, I had a bug where the enemy just walked off the platform which wasn't happening until the school wifi had a bad connection but overall, it was a fun last experience of the expo.


My takeaways for presenting in the expo are
* Have a plan b if something unexpected happens like the school wifi having a bad connection.
* Be more prepared to show off your project in a short time span.


### EDP
I am currently on the 8th stage of the Engineering Design Process and this stage is to show off my project to students/staff/alumni of HSTAT. At this time, I am technically past this stage because I finished presenting both in class and to students of HSTAT in the cafeteria. However, if someone wanted to see what I did in computer science during my senior year, I would still be on this stage showing off my project to said person.


### Skills
I enhanced my ability to have a growth mindset and got better on how I should communicate my project to different people.
#### Growth Mindset
During the expo I had many unexpected bugs because of the school wifi. Instead of complaining on the wifi and saying "oh I am sorry I can't present my project to you", I pushed through the bugs and presented the part of the project that I could so the audience had a general idea of what my project is about. It's sad that some may not see what I created but at least they are a part of what you can do with computer science instead of showing off nothing and then being discouraged to learn more about this topic.
#### Communication
I realized that in the expo, when I presented to my first audience, It wouldn't be as engaging as I thought it was going to be. When I kind of talk too fast and explain something about my project then let them try my project out, They would be confused on what to do. So I came up with a solution that would explain the basics of the game and how to control it. Then when It came up to the question, I directly looked at them and asked the question as if I am asking the audience instead of just showing off my project. I found this more engaging and better with the flow of presenting my project.
### Next Steps
It's June and the last few days of high school/senior year. It really feels unreal that I am here at this point of my life and I am thankful for everything I experienced through this 4 year course of computer science. Going to college, Although I am not majoring in computer science I will take the skills I learnt from this course and either use it to good use or enhance them to a level I would be more comfortable with.
