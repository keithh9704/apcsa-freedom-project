# APCSA Freedom Project
by **Keith Hau**

## Context
I am a student at [HSTAT](https://www.hstat.org/) in the [Software Engineering Program](https://hstatsep.github.io/). The "Freedom Project" for AP Computer Science A is a year-long project all about making anything using technology.

For my project, I chose to independently study **GDevelop** in order to help me make **A game that changes the game based on the player's decision**.

## Implications
##### PROS
* Simulates a decision that people may have to make in there lives even if the question is not that serious
  * Can see both sides of the decision while in real life u can't
    * Also exposes to the player on a different way of thinking about this decision.
* Help people to commit to a decision
##### CONS
* The question on the game is very biased towards me
* It may further make people more worried about their decisions
* People could take this game the wrong way and could negatively impact them then positively impact them.
